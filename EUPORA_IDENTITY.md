# Eupora — accepted design-stage identity

**Status:** Accepted identity; design-stage project.  
**Accepted:** September 25, 2026, by the user; recorded under DEC-0005.

The Option Space Model project is named **Eupora**, pronounced **yoo-POR-uh**
(three syllables), with lowercase project spelling `eupora`.

> Eupora develops the possibilities.

Eupora discovers, constructs, investigates, and refines meaningfully different
ways forward. It investigates constraints, feasibility, evidence, uncertainty,
tradeoffs, and missing approaches; preserves useful distinctions; and reports
gaps and bounded adequacy of the option set for a downstream decision.
Final action selection belongs to a person or a separate decision capability.
It does not claim real-world exhaustiveness or guarantee one correct answer.

The character form is inspired by Greek *euporos* and *euporia*, connecting
inventiveness and resourcefulness with available means. This is a naming
interpretation, not a literal ancient capability description or a claim of an
exact ancient deity named Eupora. The project's
[accepted naming decision](../../eupora/NAMING_DECISION.md) supplies the lexical
sources and natural usage. “Ask Eupora to develop the possibilities” is a
representative request, not evidence of an implemented runtime.

The [current project home](../../eupora/WORKSPACE.md) is `projects/eupora/`
under the Plectarium family home. Option Space Model/OSM remains descriptive
terminology and historical source identity, not Eupora's character-name acronym.

This note records an intended capability identity alongside the established
[family naming convention](standalone-capability-family-packet-v1/spec/canonical-character-identities.md).
There was no OSM registration in the family packet. This local naming addition
leaves its canonical ten-capability mapping, repository contracts, seeds,
version, and historical identity import unchanged. It does not claim a working
engine, qualification, normative member admission, family-contract publication,
or release.
