# Gnomia — accepted design-stage identity

**Status:** Accepted identity; design-stage project.  
**Accepted:** September 25, 2026, by the user; recorded under DEC-0006.

The Deliberative Decision Model project is named **Gnomia**, with intended
lowercase spelling `gnomia`. The discussed English product pronunciation is
**NOH-mee-uh**, three syllables, with a silent initial G.

The supporting identity language from the naming discussion is:

> Eupora develops the possibilities. Gnomia discerns what they justify.

The user's explicit finalization was of the name. This supporting line does
not assert separate approval of a fixed tagline.

Gnomia represents a discerning adviser who weighs reasons, tests assumptions,
and explains what would change a conclusion. Its reference design provides
bounded, evidence-aware deliberation over finite alternatives: validating the
frame and authorized preferences, assessing evidence and consequences,
applying appropriate procedures, and examining uncertainty, tradeoffs,
robustness, and challenges. Its results may recommend, conditionally recommend,
retain an unresolved frontier, abstain, request information, options, or
clarification, or report no admissible alternative within the considered set.

Facts and forecasts remain distinct from stakeholder values. Gnomia does not
invent values, weights, or agreement, and recommendations confer no permission
to act. Eupora develops and refines alternatives; Gnomia assesses the
conclusions their decision basis warrants and may propose further exploration.

The modern character form is inspired by Greek *gnōmē* (γνώμη), including
judgment, opinion, counsel, and purpose. This is a naming interpretation, not
a literal ancient capability definition or a claim of a deity named Gnomia.
The project's [accepted naming decision](../../gnomia/NAMING_DECISION.md)
records the lexical source, known prior use, boundaries, and natural usage.

The [current project home](../../gnomia/WORKSPACE.md) is `projects/gnomia/`.
Deliberative Decision Model/DDM remains descriptive and historical source
terminology, not an acronym expansion of Gnomia. The imported reference
architecture, contracts, numerical kernels, examples, and tests remain
unpromoted design material; there is no complete operational or qualified
engine and no Git checkout at this project home.
