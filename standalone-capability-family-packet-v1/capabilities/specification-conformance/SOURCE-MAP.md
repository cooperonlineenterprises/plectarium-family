## Research/provenance

The generated build packet MUST pin the exact published family Git commit,
packet version `1.2.0`, SHA-256 of that commit's `PACKET-MANIFEST.json`, and
the complete set of family and seed paths used. Missing publication identity
gates the pin; it must not be guessed or filled with a placeholder.
