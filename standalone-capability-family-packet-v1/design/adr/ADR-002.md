# ADR-002: Harness-agnostic core

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-08-31

## Context

The capability family needs a durable rule for harness-agnostic core. UCA provides the first concrete architecture, while future products will stress different execution, evidence, and security conditions.

## Decision

Capability engines do not depend on Octon or another harness.

## Consequences

- Project-generation prompts inherit this decision.
- Capability-specific exceptions require an ADR and must preserve higher family invariants.
- Validation and release criteria test the boundary where practical.

## Alternatives considered

- embed specialist logic in the harness;
- permit transport-specific semantic implementations;
- rely on conversational instructions instead of durable contracts;
- generalize through a universal framework before real implementations.

These alternatives are rejected or deferred because they increase coupling, authority ambiguity, divergence, or speculative weight.

## Reconsideration

Revisit only when evidence from implemented capabilities shows that the decision prevents legitimate domain behavior or increases total lifecycle cost. A replacement must include migration and compatibility impact.
