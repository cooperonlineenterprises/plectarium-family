## Historical versus current basis

## Research discipline

- prefer primary repositories, specifications, official documentation, and evaluation assets;
- distinguish project claims from independently reproduced evidence;
- inspect code, schemas, tests, and benchmark methodology when architecture depends on them;
- do not copy large amounts of text or source into capability projects;
- record exact versions/commits when freezing an adapter or compatibility contract.
